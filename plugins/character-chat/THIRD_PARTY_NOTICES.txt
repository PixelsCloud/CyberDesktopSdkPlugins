CyberDesktop Character Chat — public plugin

The plugin DLL uses CyberDesktop SDK, CyberCanvas, nlohmann/json and the
CyberDesktop Character Protocol library. The corresponding notices accompany
this package. Lucide notices cover the associated UI artwork.

Mao and Haru sample models retain their accompanying Live2D SDK and sample
material terms. Those terms remain separate from the MIT licenses for project
code; the plugin accesses the host's render.live2d service and does not embed
the host renderer or Cubism runtime.

The Xia Qing demonstration illustrations and animation were created for this
project. Resource hashes and generation provenance accompany the assets. The
four role templates share this demonstration artwork.

Network providers, credentials, databases, model rendering and media extraction
are services of the host application. Their implementations and third-party
notices are distributed with that application, not required to build this DLL.
