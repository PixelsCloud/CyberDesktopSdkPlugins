CyberDesktop 像素霓虹招牌 / Pixel Neon Sign / 像素霓虹招牌

Original procedural pixel lettering spells CyberDesktop on a weathered metal sign.
Cyan and magenta neon, stepped paint chips, exposed wiring and intermittent contact faults.
Full sign: 2×6 (default), 1×6. Separate minimal sign: 1×4, lettering and two short light rails.
Settings: damage, brightness, glow, backing plate, two neon colors, animation, speed and intensity.
Effects: faulty contact, breathing, light sweep, light wave, signal glitch, cyber mix (default).
Animation pauses while hidden and stops when disabled or intensity is zero.
Faulty-contact mode also stops when damage is zero. Continuous effects run at up to 30 FPS.
Only changed illumination requests a redraw; no network, audio or external font dependencies.
Rendered through the shared SDK Canvas at the host's actual DPI with antialiasing.
Original lettering and sign artwork: CyberDesktop. Dependency notices: licenses/.

Both backings fill the actual brick content bounds, with standard 4 px descriptor padding.
Lettering scales uniformly to preserve square pixels. Default damage: 90%; default speed: 1.25x.
Contact/glitch cycle: 3.6 seconds at 1x speed (2.88 seconds at default speed).
