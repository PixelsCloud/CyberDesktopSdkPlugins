CyberDesktop Mechanical Pixels artwork

The four chassis sprites were generated with OpenAI's built-in image_gen from
the project's approved mechanical-pixel-clock-music-concept.png design.
They contain static housing only; time, matrix cells and spectrum are rendered
from runtime data. Source prompts and per-asset provenance accompany the source.
No third-party branded artwork or fonts are embedded in these chassis images.

CyberDesktop SDK and CyberCanvas are used under their included MIT notices.
Yoga and Tweeny notices for the existing linked shared implementation are
included in each plugin package's licenses directory.
