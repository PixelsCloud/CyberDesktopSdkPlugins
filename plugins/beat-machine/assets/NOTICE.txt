CyberDesktop precision physics mechanism materials

Generated with OpenAI image_gen using design/mechanical-physics-precision-redesign.png.
The 4x2 and 6x2 chassis were authored separately. Mechanism PNGs are independent
transparent layers. Digits, physics, tracks, springs and collision response remain live.
Original PNG dimensions, layout and SHA-256 hashes are recorded in provenance.json.
The renderer registers visible PNG bounds and exact mechanical pivot coordinates,
preserves circular parts, and decodes the chassis for its actual physical display size.
Only the chassis for this independent plugin size is shipped.
Source prompts: tools/PrecisionAssetPrompts.txt in the source tree.
Engine and Box2D licenses are included separately in the package.
